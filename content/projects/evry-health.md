## Overview

Evry Health is a health plan built around a simpler member experience. Its web presence has two jobs: explaining the plan clearly to prospective members and employers, and giving current members a portal to manage their care.

## The work

I worked on Evry Health's production web platform and member portal, where getting the UI right directly affects whether people can understand and use their health coverage.

## What mattered

- **Clarity.** Healthcare is confusing. The interface has to make plan details and next steps easy to follow.
- **Accessibility.** Members come from every background and use every kind of device and assistive technology.
- **Reliability.** A member portal is a production system people depend on to get care.

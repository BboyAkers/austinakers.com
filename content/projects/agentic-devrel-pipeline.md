## Overview

Developer relations runs on technical content: tutorials, guides, example apps, and enablement material that stays in sync with a fast-moving product. At Harper, producing that content by hand was the bottleneck.

As Head of Developer Relations, I architected and deployed an **autonomous five-agent LLM pipeline** that streamlines how technical content is generated, so the team could spend its time on review, community, and strategy instead of first drafts.

## Approach

- **Specialized agents, not one big prompt.** The work is split across five cooperating agents, each responsible for a distinct stage of producing a piece of content.
- **Built around review.** The pipeline produces work for humans to approve, keeping technical accuracy and Harper's voice under editorial control.
- **Grounded in the product.** Output is tied to real Harper APIs and example code, the same material behind Harper's tutorials and templates.

## Outcome

- **60%+ reduction** in content delivery time and turnaround.
- More capacity for the rest of the role: developer education, community strategy, technical onboarding demos for enterprise partners, and hosting the Harper *SELECT \** podcast.
